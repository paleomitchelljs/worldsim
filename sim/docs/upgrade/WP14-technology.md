# WP14: technology that can be lost and has costs

**Goal.** Technology that a people can lose, borrow slowly or not at all,
and pay for, so that peoples differ in what they know.

**Evidence** (open-questions.md, "Economy and technology").
- **Knowledge is never lost.** With `supply`, a technology's *use*
  lapses without ore or cities, but the knowledge stays.
- **Low-innovation peoples still reach steel** through long contact.
  Borrowing runs at (0.3 + innovation) (`technology.py`,
  `cities.py`), never zero.
- **Innovation, seafaring and trade cost nothing** in their own right.
- Steel as the final technology over 8,000 years is intended (U,
  2026-11-09). Any change here can push it past the end of a run.

Read first: `wsim/technology.py`, `docs/rules/` on technology,
`structural.md` ("Supply and loss of technology").

## Stage A: design note (needs U)

1. **Loss.** Knowledge that decays when unused for generations, or
   when a people loses its cities (the Bronze Age collapse case).
2. **Borrowing.** Whether the floor of 0.3 should go, so that a closed
   people can refuse a technology.
3. **Costs.** What innovation, seafaring and trade cost: food, cohesion
   or something else.
4. For each, what it does to the timeline (P2c first bronze; steel).
